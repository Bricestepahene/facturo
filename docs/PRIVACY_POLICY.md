# Privacy Policy — Facturo

**Last updated: June 2024**

## Overview

Facturo is a mobile invoicing application designed to work entirely offline. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

**Facturo collects NO personal data.**

All data you enter (clients, products, invoices, quotes) is stored exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database. This data is never transmitted to any server or third party by Facturo itself.

## Advertising

The free version of Facturo displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** and its mediation partners (Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, ironSource). These ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. This data collection is governed by their respective privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/policy.php)
- [AppLovin Privacy Policy](https://www.applovin.com/privacy/)

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings:
- Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

## In-App Purchases

Pro upgrade purchases are processed by **Google Play Billing**. Payment information is handled exclusively by Google and is never accessible to Facturo. We receive only a purchase confirmation token.

## PDF Sharing

When you share a PDF, the file is transferred via the sharing mechanism you choose (WhatsApp, Gmail, etc.). Facturo does not intercept or store these transfers.

## Backups

Backup files you export contain your business data in JSON format. These files are saved to your device and shared only when you explicitly trigger the share action. Facturo does not upload backup files to any server.

## Data Deletion

To delete all Facturo data, uninstall the application. All local data is removed with the app.

## Children

Facturo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Contact

For privacy questions: **privacy@facturo.app**

## Changes

This policy may be updated. Significant changes will be noted in app release notes.

---

*Facturo is committed to your privacy. Your business data belongs to you.*
